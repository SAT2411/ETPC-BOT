# Privacy Policy — ETPC Group Bots

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **ETPC Group Bots** (such as **ETPC Core**, **ETPC Support**, and other official bots operated by the English Test Prep Community, collectively referred to as **"we"**, **"us"**, or the **"Bots"**) collect, use, store, and protect information from Discord users and servers.

---

### 1. Information We Collect
ETPC Group Bots collect only the data necessary to deliver moderation, user safety, and support functionality:

1. **Discord Identifiers & Metadata:**
   - User IDs, usernames/tags, Guild (Server) IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs, and action timestamps required to attribute tickets, sanctions, and permissions accurately.
2. **Recorded User Messages (Moderation & User Safety):**
   - **Support, Report & Appeal Tickets:** When a user opens or participates in a ticket channel, our Bots record the messages and metadata exchanged in that ticket channel to generate support transcripts.
   - **Moderation & Safety Context:** Command inputs, sanction reasons, staff notes, closure summaries, and optional user feedback/ratings submitted via slash commands or interactive modals.
   - *Why we record this:* Recording these messages is strictly for beneficial purposes—specifically **moderation accountability, user safety, abuse investigation, dispute resolution, and quality assurance**.
   - *Scope Limitation:* We do **not** spy on private Direct Messages (DMs), collect passwords or payment details, or indiscriminately log general public chat outside of designated ticket, moderation, and audit workflows.
3. **Voice & Moderation Audit Logs:**
   - Server moderation events (such as warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, and moderator-initiated voice moves/disconnects/mutes) to maintain accurate server audit trails.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively for:
- Operating support, user report, and ban/sanction appeal ticket systems and providing downloadable transcripts.
- Protecting community members and enforcing server rules and safety standards.
- Maintaining verifiable moderation logs (`modlogs`) and preventing abuse or unauthorized actions.
- Auditing and improving staff response quality.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Hosting Infrastructure
We take the security of your data seriously and do our utmost to protect all stored records from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:
- **Industry-Standard Cloud Security:** All bot services and databases are hosted on **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI)** and industry-standard secure cloud servers protected by network firewalls, encrypted connections (TLS/SSL), and strict SSH key-based authentication.
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Access to stored moderation logs and ticket transcripts is strictly restricted to authorized server administrators and designated moderation staff.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure
- **No Sale of Data:** We **never** sell, rent, trade, or monetize user data or recorded messages.
- **No Unauthorized Third Parties:** Data is never shared with third-party advertisers or external organizations, except where strictly required by law or to comply with Discord Trust & Safety investigations.

---

### 5. Data Retention
- Active configuration data (such as module destinations and role permissions) is retained while the Bots remain in the server.
- Ticket transcripts, moderation cases, and voice audit logs are retained in accordance with our operational safety and audit retention policies or until removed by authorized server administrators.

---

### 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion Requests
Users have the right to inquire about their stored data or request the deletion of personal records where applicable (subject to legitimate moderation and safety record-keeping requirements to prevent ban evasion or repeat abuse).

To submit a privacy inquiry or data deletion request, please contact the ETPC administration team by opening a support ticket in our official Discord server or reaching out to the server owners directly with your Discord User ID.

---

### 7. Updates to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements in our security practices or bot features. Any updates will be published in this repository with a revised "Last Updated" date.
